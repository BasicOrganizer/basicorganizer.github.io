# Privacy Policy for Basic Cycle

**Last Updated: July 18, 2026**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Basic Cycle ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. Basic Cycle is a personal period and menstrual cycle tracker.

Basic Cycle is designed to keep everything you enter **on your own device**. The app has no account, no server, no cloud storage, no analytics, and no advertising. **Your information never leaves your device** unless you personally choose to share it (for example, by sending us a feedback email).

## Medical Disclaimer

## Health Information

Information you enter into Basic Cycle — including menstrual cycles, period days, ovulation, symptoms, moods, notes, intimacy (sexual activity), and other health-related information — may be considered sensitive personal information under certain laws.

This information remains stored **only on your device** and is **never transmitted to the developer** or to any third party.

Health-related information is used **solely** to provide tracking, calendar, journal, statistics, and other functionality within the app. It is **never** used for advertising, profiling, analytics, marketing, or sold or shared with anyone.

Basic Cycle does **not** use artificial intelligence or external services to analyze your health information. All calculations happen locally on your device.

Basic Cycle is **not** a healthcare provider, health insurer, or other entity covered by the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), and this policy is not a HIPAA notice.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally

All user data is stored **locally on your device**. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

- **Cycle Data:** Days marked as period days and days marked as ovulation.
- **Symptoms:** Symptoms you log on specific dates, including any custom symptoms you create.
- **Intimacy Records:** Days you choose to mark intimacy (sexual activity).
- **Notes:** Free-text notes associated with specific dates.
- **Personalization Settings:** Your typical cycle length, period length, and prediction preferences.
- **App Preferences:** Settings such as language, first day of the week, and whether you have acknowledged the in-app disclaimer.

This information is stored in a local database and local app preferences on your device. It is **not transmitted to any external servers, to the developer, or to any third parties**. We have **no ability to see, access, or recover** the data you enter into the app.

### Future Features

The app currently collects no information off your device. If future versions introduce features that require collecting or transmitting information, this Privacy Policy will be updated **before** those features become available, and any feature involving sensitive health information would require your **explicit opt-in consent**.

### Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or store on any external server:

- Your cycle data, symptoms, intimacy records, or notes
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Precise or approximate location data
- Contact information
- Photos, media, or data from other apps
- Any device identifiers or advertising identifiers

The only time your email address becomes visible to us is if **you choose** to contact us or send feedback by email (see "Feedback and Support Emails" below).

## Data Security

- **Local Storage:** The app is designed to keep information stored locally on your device.
- **No Transmission:** The app does not send your data off your device.
- **No Automatic Backup:** The app disables automatic cloud backup and device-transfer of its data (`android:allowBackup="false"`, with backup and data-extraction rules configured to exclude the app's data).
- **Device Security:** Because your data lives on your device, its protection depends on your device's own security features. We recommend enabling a screen lock and device encryption, keeping your device updated, and not sharing your device with others.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. The developer cannot recover information that is deleted, lost, or becomes unavailable because of device failure, reset, loss, damage, or app removal. You are responsible for any backups you choose to make of your device.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- All data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there.
- You can delete or unmark individual entries (period days, ovulation, intimacy, symptoms, and notes) at any time within the app.
