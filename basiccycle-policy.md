# Privacy Policy for Basic Cycle

**Last Updated: July 18, 2026**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Basic Cycle ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. Basic Cycle is a personal period and menstrual cycle tracker. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Because a period tracker can involve information about your menstrual cycle, sexual activity, and general well-being, we have designed the app so that this information stays **on your own device** and is **never sent to us or to anyone else**.

## Medical Disclaimer

Basic Cycle is a personal tracking and informational tool. It is **not a medical device**, and the developer is **not a medical professional**.

- The app does **not** provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- Predictions, fertile-window estimates, ovulation estimates, and statistics are **approximate** and are based only on the data you enter. They may be inaccurate, especially with irregular cycles.
- The fertile window shown in the app is for **general orientation only**. It is still possible to become pregnant on days outside any predicted fertile window.
- The app is **not a contraceptive** and **must not be relied upon to prevent or achieve pregnancy**.
- Always consult a qualified healthcare provider regarding your health, fertility, contraception, or any medical concerns.

You use the app and any information it displays at your own discretion and risk.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally

All user data is stored **locally on your device**. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

- **Cycle Data:** Days marked as period days and days marked as ovulation.
- **Symptoms:** Symptoms you log on specific dates, including any custom symptoms you create.
- **Intimacy Records:** Days you choose to mark intimacy (sexual activity).
- **Notes:** Free-text notes associated with specific dates.
- **Personalization Settings:** Your typical cycle length, period length, and prediction preferences (for example, whether predictions use your averages or your tracked ovulation).
- **App Preferences:** Settings such as language, first day of the week, and whether you have acknowledged the in-app disclaimer.

This information is stored in a local SQLite database and local app preferences on your device. It is **not transmitted to any external servers, to the developer, or to any third parties**.

We have **no ability to see, access, or recover** the data you enter into the app.

### Third-Party Services

Currently, the app does **not** use advertisements, analytics, tracking technologies, accounts, or cloud storage. It requests **no special device permissions** and does **not** require an internet connection to function.

In the future, optional features **may** be introduced:

- **Advertisements:** If ever added, device and usage information may be collected by third-party ad networks. Your cycle data, symptoms, intimacy records, and notes will **never** be shared with advertisers.
- **Analytics:** Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics may be collected to improve app performance. Sensitive health and cycle content will **never** be included.
- **Cloud Storage & Sync:** Optional backup and synchronization between devices. Would require explicit opt-in.
- **Cross-App Integration:** Optional sharing of data between authorized apps. Would require explicit consent.

All future features will be clearly disclosed in app updates or within the app interface, and users will have the option to **opt-in or opt-out**. Any feature involving sensitive health data would require **explicit, informed opt-in consent**.

### Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or store on any external server:

- Your cycle data, symptoms, intimacy records, or notes
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Precise or approximate location data
- Contact information
- Photos, media, or data from other apps
- Any device identifiers or advertising identifiers

The only time your email address becomes visible to us is if **you choose** to contact us or send feedback by email. In that case, we receive only what you include in your message (see "Feedback and Support Emails" below).

## Data Security

- **Local Storage:** All your data is stored in a local SQLite database and local app preferences on your device.
- **No Transmission:** The app does not send your data off your device, so it is not exposed to network interception by the app.
- **No Automatic Backup:** The app disables automatic cloud backup and device-transfer of its data (`android:allowBackup="false"`, with backup and data-extraction rules configured to exclude the app's data).
- **Device Security:** Because your data lives on your device, its protection depends on your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption, keeping your device updated, and not sharing it with others). We recommend enabling a screen lock and device encryption.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data stored on your device.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- All data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there.
- You can delete or unmark individual entries (period days, ovulation, intimacy, symptoms, and notes) at any time within the app.
- You can delete custom symptoms you have created.

- There is **no server-side storage**, so there is no separate copy of your data for us to retain or delete.

## User Rights

You have full control over your data at all times. You can access, modify, and delete your entries directly within the app, and you can remove all data by uninstalling the app.

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data on any server, most data-subject requests can be fulfilled entirely by you, on your device.

- **EEA / Norway (GDPR):** You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** You have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information.
- **Other regions:** You may have similar rights under your local data protection laws.

### Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

- You can delete individual entries, symptoms, and notes within the app.
- You can uninstall the app to remove all local data.
- Since we do not store your data on our servers, we generally hold no personal data about you to erase. If you have contacted us by email, you may request deletion of that correspondence.
