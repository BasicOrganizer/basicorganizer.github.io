# Privacy Policy for Basic Journal

**Last Updated: July 25, 2026**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Basic Journal ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. Basic Journal is a personal journaling app for reflections, gratitude entries, and mood logging.

Basic Journal is designed to keep everything you enter **on your own device**. The app has no account, no server, no cloud storage, no analytics, and no advertising. The app does **not** request Internet access. **Your journal content never leaves your device** unless you personally choose to share it (for example, by exporting a file yourself, or by sending us a feedback email).

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally

All user data is stored **locally on your device**. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

- **Reflections / diary entries:** Free-text journal entries and drafts you write.
- **Gratitude entries:** Items you list as things you are grateful for.
- **Mood entries:** Mood values you log, with timestamps and related statistics generated on-device.
- **Reminder preferences:** Which reminders you enable and the times you choose.
- **App lock settings:** Whether app lock is enabled, a **hashed** representation of your PIN (not the PIN itself in plain text), related salt, and whether biometric unlock is enabled.
- **App preferences:** Settings such as language, theme, start screen, date/time formats, and mood color palette.

This information is stored in a local database and local app preferences on your device. It is **not transmitted to any external servers, to the developer, or to any third parties**. We have **no ability to see, access, or recover** the data you enter into the app.

### Optional Export Created by You

You may choose to **export** selected journal data to a text file using your device’s system file picker. That file is created **only when you request it**, and is saved where **you** choose on your device or cloud storage linked to your device.

Once you export a file, that copy is under **your** control. Exported files are no longer protected by the app’s security features once created. The app does not upload exports to the developer. If you store or share an exported file outside the app (for example in cloud storage or by sending it to someone), that is outside the app’s control and subject to the policies of those services or recipients.

### Future Features

The app currently does not collect information off your device. If future versions introduce features that require collecting or transmitting information, this Privacy Policy will be updated **before** those features become available, and any feature involving sensitive personal information would require your **explicit opt-in consent**.

### Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or store on any external server:

- Your reflections, gratitude entries, mood logs, or other journal content
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Precise or approximate location data
- Contact information
- Photos, media, or data from other apps
- Advertising identifiers or analytics identifiers
- Biometric templates (fingerprints, face data, etc.)

The only time your email address becomes visible to us is if **you choose** to contact us or send feedback by email (see "Feedback and Support Emails" below).

|---|---|
| **Notifications** | To show optional journal reminders you schedule. |

These permissions are used **locally on your device**. They are **not** used to collect or transmit your journal content. You can decline notification or alarm-related permissions; reminders that depend on them will not work until granted. App lock and biometrics are entirely optional.

The app does **not** require Internet permission and is designed to work offline for its core journaling features.

## App Lock and Biometrics

If you enable **app lock**:

- You create a PIN that is stored on your device only as a **salted cryptographic hash**. The app is designed so that your PIN is not stored in plain text.
- If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is handled by Android’s biometric system. The app receives only a success/failure result and does **not** access fingerprint, face, or other biometric templates.
- App lock helps reduce casual access on your device. It is **not** a guarantee against all forms of unauthorized access (for example, a compromised or rooted device, malware, or physical access with forensic tools).

You are responsible for choosing a PIN you can remember. If you forget your PIN and cannot unlock the app, we **cannot** recover your journal data for you, because we never receive a copy of it.

## Data Security

- **Local Storage:** The app is designed to keep information stored locally on your device.
- **No Transmission by the App:** The app does not send your journal data off your device.
- **No Automatic Backup:** The app disables automatic cloud backup and device-transfer of its data (`android:allowBackup="false"`, with backup and data-extraction rules configured to exclude the app’s data). This helps keep sensitive journal content from being copied automatically by system backup tools.
- **Optional App Lock:** You may enable a PIN and optional biometrics for additional on-device protection.
- **Device Security:** Because your data lives on your device, its protection depends on your device’s own security features. We recommend enabling a screen lock and device encryption, keeping your device updated, not sharing your unlocked device with others, and using app lock if others may have access to your phone.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. The developer cannot recover information that is deleted, lost, or becomes unavailable because of device failure, reset, loss, damage, forgotten PIN, or app removal. You are responsible for any backups you choose to make (for example, via the in-app export feature).

- All data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there.
